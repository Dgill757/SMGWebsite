# SummitOS Command Center Master Specification

This is the durable product contract for SummitOS. A feature is not complete because a card exists. It is complete only when its data is live, its action is safe, its result is evidenced, and Dan can use it from desktop, Telegram, SMS, or phone where appropriate.

## North star

Build a CEO operating system for a solo founder that turns live business truth into the next best revenue action. SummitOS must help Dan reach $10K, $25K, $50K, $85K, and $100K MRR without inventing data or silently performing irreversible actions.

## Non-negotiable truth and safety

- Current business truth comes from canonical live records, not historical reports.
- Distinguish reported employees from verified employees with fresh run evidence.
- Every meaningful agent run records timestamps, inputs, tools, artifacts, counts, cost, duration, blockers, freshness, and confidence.
- Email, SMS, social publishing, calendar writes, CRM mutations, file writes, and computer actions require a preview, approval, and receipt unless Dan explicitly enables a narrowly scoped automation.
- Automated prospect outreach remains paused until explicitly resumed.
- Never invent employee counts, prospect facts, completed actions, sources, or financial outcomes.

## Command Center navigation

- Jarvis: multimodal conversation, approvals, receipts, voice stop, live system state.
- Brief: grounded morning brief, cash-impact priorities, calendar, inbox, agent blockers, daily work plan.
- Goals: milestone selector, funnel calculator, pace tracker, daily catch-up coach, activity ledger, executive coach.
- Team: executive cabinet, org chart, verified employee directory, run evidence, blockers, costs, artifacts.
- Pipeline: scraped prospects, smart lists, research profiles, PageSpeed/SEO audit, scripts, notes, GHL sync, call outcomes.
- Content: research-backed drafts, images, channel readiness, approval and publishing receipts.
- Clients: active revenue truth, health, risks, deliverables, retention actions, expansion opportunities.
- Demos: safe demo creation and delivery approval; delivery is never preselected.
- Finance: P&L, expenses, runway, pricing scenarios, cash forecasting, recurring and setup revenue.
- Intel: sourced market, competitor, product, advertising, and roofing-industry intelligence.

## Goals operating hub

- Select $10K, $25K, $50K, $85K, or $100K MRR with one click; custom targets remain supported.
- Show current MRR, gap, progress, clients required, recurring revenue gained, setup cash, dials, conversations, bookings, held demos, proposals, and expected closes.
- All math derives from editable price, setup fee, connect rate, booking rate, show rate, close rate, workdays, and deadline.
- Separate assumptions from actual observed conversion rates.
- Track daily dials, conversations, bookings, shows, proposals, follow-ups, content, clients, MRR, and setup cash.
- If yesterday misses plan, distribute the remaining requirement across remaining workdays and clearly state today's catch-up number.
- Show best case, base case, and conservative scenarios without presenting projections as guarantees.
- Celebrate completed milestones, show streaks, identify the funnel constraint, and prescribe the next action.
- Provide an embedded Jarvis growth coach grounded in the selected milestone and actual ledger.
- Deliver daily reminders through dashboard and approved notification channels.

## Executive cabinet

- CEO: strategy, prioritization, pricing, offers, partnerships, goal pacing, and executive synthesis.
- CRO: pipeline, prospecting, call lists, scripts, conversion improvement, follow-up, and sales coaching.
- CMO: positioning, competitor intelligence, content, ads, channel strategy, and demand generation.
- COO: workflow reliability, schedules, capacity, handoffs, blockers, and operating cadence.
- CTO: product roadmap, architecture, security, integrations, technical debt, and new sellable automation ideas.
- CFO: P&L, runway, pricing, margins, budget decisions, cash scenarios, and cost reduction.
- Client Success: health, onboarding, outcomes, retention, reviews, risks, and expansion.

Each executive governs named specialist workflows, may propose assignments, and must cite live evidence. Executives do not become fictional characters with fake activity.

## Prospect revenue workflow

- Scrape leads and add them to GHL while automated outreach is paused.
- Build a profile for every lead: source, contact information, location, website, employee count only when sourced, ratings, reviews, marketing footprint, PageSpeed, SEO, accessibility, best-practices score, likely pain, offer fit, and confidence.
- Produce cold-call opener, complete script, voicemail, email, SMS, discovery questions, objection handling, meeting brief, and recommended offer.
- Support saved smart lists, call queues, call disposition, notes, reminders, and GHL note/contact synchronization.
- Research requests such as “find 10 Tulsa roofers without websites and 10 weak websites” produce a reviewable list, never automatic outreach.

## Daily operating cadence

- Morning: live brief to dashboard, Slack, and approved email destination.
- Workday: revenue plan, priority call list, inbox triage, meetings, follow-ups, content approval, and client risks.
- Evening: actual-versus-plan report, missed-work catch-up calculation, artifacts, blockers, and tomorrow’s first action.
- Weekly: executive review covering revenue, funnel, client health, marketing, product opportunities, costs, reliability, and experiments.

## Premium Roofing Demo Standard

- Existing-site path: Firecrawl captures content and image candidates; the model router extracts brand facts and verbatim proof. Real logos and imagery take priority.
- No-site path: CRM facts seed the concept. Unknown history, credentials, reviews, guarantees, response times, and project proof stay unknown.
- Every result visibly says it is a website concept, and curated stock photography is never represented as completed client work.
- Preview forms do not transmit data. CRM wiring happens only after approval.
- Available directions are Premium Modern, Bold Editorial, and Heritage and Trust.
- Deployment is blocked unless the generated document passes identity, disclosure, mobile, accessibility, semantic, conversion, fabrication, and document-depth checks.

## Definition of done

“Ready to run the company” means a complete acceptance run succeeds from a clean start, all displayed numbers trace to authoritative sources, all actions have receipts, voice works without overlapping playback, agent work has evidence, and no channel claims an action it did not complete.
