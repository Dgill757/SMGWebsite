The Golden Rule
Research shows section-by-section beats one giant prompt every time. Use the Foundation prompt first to establish project skeleton, then build section by section. Giant prompts cause "context collapse" — the AI makes too many decisions at once and quality tanks.

The 3-Layer Prompt Method
Research shows AI is better at structure than aesthetics. Use three layers in order: 1) Structure first (what goes where), 2) Styling second (colors, spacing, typography), 3) Functionality third (animations, interactions, forms). Never specify all three simultaneously.

Context Management (Peter Yang's Rules)

The Tailwind v4 Trap
Tailwind upgraded to v4 in January 2025. Most AI models only know v3 and will produce broken code. Ask: "Which version of Tailwind CSS do you know?" before starting any build. This single tip prevents hours of debugging.
